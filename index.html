The studio & the founder

Somewhere in your business, a person is the integration between two systems.

That's the work Wisqora removes - the copy-paste between tools that never learned to talk, the weekly report rebuilt by hand, the onboarding email sent one at a time. We replace it with processes that run on their own, and we hand you the keys.

I spent 14+ years as a DevOps & cloud engineer inside VodafoneZiggo, ABN AMRO, Knab Digital Bank, SurePay and General Electric - hired to solve exactly this problem at enterprise scale, under banking-grade security and compliance. Wisqora brings that playbook to businesses that need the same leverage without the enterprise overhead.

It's a lean model on purpose: one accountable lead on every engagement - me - with vetted specialists brought in per project. No idle bench, no account managers, honest scoping (including telling you when something is a two-day fix).

Proof before scope. We automate one real, expensive task in production and measure it before pricing the larger build.
You own everything. Every project ends with documentation, access and a runbook - the system runs without us.
